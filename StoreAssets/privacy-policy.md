# Drain Run Privacy Policy

Effective date: [the day you publish this]

## Summary

Drain Run has no accounts, sign-in, in-app purchases or analytics. From version 1.10, it shows optional video ads: after a game over, you can choose to watch one to revive once per run. You never have to watch an ad to play.

The ads come from Unity (Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and its affiliate ironSource Ads). To show them, Unity collects some data from your device, as described below.

## What the game keeps

Your best score, your settings (sound and vibration) and your ad privacy choice are saved only on your device. The developer does not receive or store any of it. Uninstalling Drain Run, or clearing its data, deletes it.

## Ads and the data our ad partners collect

To load and show ads, the ad software in Drain Run collects information from your device and sends it to Unity and the ad networks it works with, such as:

- Device identifiers, including your device's advertising ID
- Your IP address, which can show your general location (like your country or city), not your precise location
- Device and app details, such as the phone model, operating system, language and app version
- Ad activity, such as which ads were loaded, shown, watched or tapped
- Diagnostic information, such as errors and performance data from the ad software

They use it to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ads, prevent fraud and, if allowed, show ads that suit you. How Unity uses, shares and keeps this data is explained in its privacy policy:

https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

The developer does not receive this data, only totals such as how many ads were shown. Drain Run does not sell your personal information.

## Your choices

- **EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland:** before any ads load, Drain Run asks whether our ad partners may use your data to show ads that suit you. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change your answer any time from AD PRIVACY on the main screen.
- **Everywhere:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
- **Ads are optional:** you only see an ad when you choose to watch one.

## Children

Drain Run is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If Drain Run's data practices change, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
